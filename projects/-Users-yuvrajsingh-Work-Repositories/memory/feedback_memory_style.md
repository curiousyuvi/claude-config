Don't dump full resume, address, phone, education history, or internship details into memory files. When user shares a LinkedIn PDF or portfolio link, extract only the specific facts needed (product names, links, role titles) — not the whole document.

**Why:** User explicitly said "I gave the linkedin PDF just for exact product and my experience name and links" after I wrote their full career history + personal contact info into memory. They made me revert it.

**How to apply:** When the user shares a profile document, ask what specific details to extract rather than saving everything. Keep memory entries focused on what's actionable for future sessions, not a comprehensive dossier.
