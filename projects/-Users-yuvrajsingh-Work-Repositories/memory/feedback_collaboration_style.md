For multi-session / multi-phase work, Yuvraj wants to be involved in the *whole* development phase, not just final review.

**Why:** He needs to understand what's happening well enough to (a) answer his CTO's questions, (b) push back on direction, (c) take CTO discussion points back upstream. Black-box "I did the thing, here's the result" leaves him unable to do those things.

**How to apply:**
- In plans, explain *why* each technical choice, not just *what*. The plan is a teaching artifact, not just a spec.
- Phase work into checkpoints with explicit review gates — don't run 4 phases in one go.
- Maintain a running list of "open questions for CTO / stakeholders" alongside the plan. Surface ambiguities in stakeholder briefs rather than silently resolving them.
- When implementing, narrate decisions inline — what was tried, what was rejected, what surprised you.
- Treat his "decisive" answers to questions as inputs to discuss, not gospel — if a choice has real downsides, flag them so he can re-litigate with the CTO.
