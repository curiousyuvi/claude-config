Chosen after extensive research (7+ ideas evaluated) on 2026-04-03 as the indie SaaS to build.

**Why:** Deepest AI moat among candidates. No standalone tool with active continuity enforcement exists — every AI story bible is locked inside a writing platform (Sudowrite, NovelCrafter, Novarrium). Writers using Scrivener/Google Docs/Word have zero options.

**How to apply:** This is the primary SaaS product goal. Plan exported to `/Work/Repositories/Lorekeeper-indie-app.md` with full competitive analysis, tech stack, pricing, and MVP scope. No code started yet.

Key details:
- One-liner: standalone AI story bible that auto-extracts, auto-updates, and actively checks continuity
- Target audience: web novelists, indie book series authors, webcomic creators, TTRPG GMs
- Pricing: Free / $12/mo (Creator) / $20/mo (Pro)
- Tech: Next.js + Supabase + pgvector + Claude API
- MVP scope: ~5-7 weeks (extraction → continuity checker → "ask your story" → polish)
- Validation step recommended before coding: Reddit posts in r/writing, r/fantasywriters
