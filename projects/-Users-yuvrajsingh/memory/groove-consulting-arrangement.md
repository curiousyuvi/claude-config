Groove Networks LLC (Delaware) acqui-hired Kroto Kreator Labs Pvt Ltd via an
Asset Purchase Agreement dated Jan 27 2025. Yuvraj was CTO/co-founder of Kroto
alongside Rose Kamal Love. Contract PDF: `~/Downloads/AcquisitionAgreement.pdf`.

Key terms that shape what side income is possible:

- Yuvraj is an **independent contractor**, not an employee. SOW #1 pays $3,000/mo
  flat, unchanged since Jan 2025. He bears all Indian tax and compliance himself.
- No exclusivity or moonlighting clause. Outside work is allowed as long as it is
  not competitive.
- Non-compete (APA 5.05) is limited to knowledge base and customer support SaaS
  in the US. Runs until the later of Feb 2027 or one year after the consulting
  agreement ends.
- IP assignment (Consulting Agmt 2a) covers only inventions arising out of the
  Services or reflecting Groove proprietary info, not everything he builds.
- Purchase price was $300k to the Kroto entity: $200k at closing, $50k on
  Schedule 1.04(b) milestones, $50k on the first anniversary (Feb 2026).
